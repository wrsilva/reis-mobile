# Android: Google Play

Play policy compliance, Play Billing upgrades and the Engage SDK.

| Guide | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [play-policy-insights](play/play-policy-insights/GUIDE.md) | Automated auditor designed to verify Android applications against Google Play Policy domains. |
| [play-billing-library-version-upgrade](play/play-billing-library-version-upgrade/GUIDE.md) | Use this skill when upgrading or migrating an Android project from any legacy Google Play Billing Library (PBL) version to the latest stable version of PBL. |
| [engage-sdk-integration](play/engage-sdk-integration/GUIDE.md) | Helps developers integrate, debug, and resolve Play Engage SDK implementation issues. |

Each guide lives in its own folder with its references and scripts. Relative paths in a guide — and `<repo_root>` where a guide uses it — refer to that guide's folder.
